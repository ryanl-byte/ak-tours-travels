AK Tours & Travels — three logo directions

One motif each, no stacking. All three share the same Fraunces wordmark (converted to outlines, so the files render identically everywhere). Direction A is implemented on the site as the default — swapping to B or C is a one-line change.

A

The Gateway

The Gateway of India’s arch, drawn as a doorway with the AK monogram standing inside it. The Gateway is where a Mumbai visit begins — and where Pooja’s Elephanta ferry leaves from. An arch is also simply a welcome.

For: Unmistakably Mumbai without being a postcard; the monogram lives inside the mark, so it works alone as a favicon; geometric, so it survives tiny sizes and print.

Against: The quietest of the three — its character comes from the serif letters, not the motif.

B

The Rickshaw

A Mumbai auto in side profile — black body, marigold canopy, the way they actually paint them. The friendliest and most street-level of the three.
